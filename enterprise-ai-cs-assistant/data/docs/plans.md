# Plans

CloudBoard offers three subscription plans.

## Starter

Starter is for small teams. It includes 5 seats, basic boards, and email support.
Monthly list price is $29.

## Pro

Pro is for growing teams. It includes 25 seats, advanced reporting, SSO, and chat support.
Monthly list price is $99.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is for larger organizations. Seats are custom, with audit logs, a dedicated CSM, and a signed contract.
Pricing is quoted by sales.
